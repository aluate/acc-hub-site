ACC Hub

ACC Hub is the internal scheduling tool of Advanced Custom Cabinets, Inc. (Hayden and Coeur d'Alene, Idaho). It runs on the company's own computers. It keeps the company's install, delivery and punch schedule on Google Calendar and shares job-site documents and photos through Google Drive, so our crews and project managers see the same schedule.

It is not a public service and has no sign-up. Only Advanced Custom Cabinets staff and the crews we work with use it.
