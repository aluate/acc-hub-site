ACC Hub privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

ACC Hub is the internal scheduling tool of Advanced Custom Cabinets, Inc. ("ACC", "we"). This policy explains what ACC Hub does with information, including the information it reaches through Google.

Who uses it

ACC Hub is used only by ACC staff and the installation crews and subcontractors we work with. It has no public sign-up and is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses through Google

ACC Hub signs in to one Google account owned by ACC. With that account's permission it:

  • Google Calendar: creates and updates ACC's own schedule calendars (installs, deliveries, punch work, field measures) and shares them with the staff and crews ACC chooses.
  • Google Drive: creates job folders in ACC's Drive, places job documents there (drawings, specifications, punch lists), and copies photos, videos and marked-up drawings that staff and crews upload to those folders back to ACC's own records.

ACC Hub only works with the calendars and folders it creates for ACC. It does not read anyone's personal email, contacts or other files.

What we keep and why

Job information (client name, site address, schedule dates, documents, job-site photos, and the names of the people who uploaded them) is kept in ACC's business records so we can build, deliver and install our clients' cabinets and handle warranty work. The Google sign-in is stored encrypted on the ACC computer that runs ACC Hub.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent or trade any information. We do not use it for advertising. We share it only with the staff, crews and subcontractors working on a job, and as the law requires.

Google API Services

ACC Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Anyone ACC has shared a calendar or folder with can ask us to remove their access, or to remove photos they uploaded, at the address below. ACC can disconnect ACC Hub from Google at any time; the account owner can also remove its access in their Google Account settings.
